    Waiting for students

    Inspect a board or check a submitted claim. Students receive a celebration or encouragement, with an optional incorrect-mark count—never which squares were incorrect. Claims are checked against the selected winning pattern; the center is free.

    Optional: shares only a count, never which squares. Zero incorrect marks can still mean the winning pattern is incomplete.

    After the first confirmed MATHO, boards lock while you check any other claims from the same draw. Reveal the review when all claims are checked; this closes further claims. New Round unlocks play.

    MATHO callers always appear at the top.

    Three incorrect MATHO claims lock that student’s board until New Round.
