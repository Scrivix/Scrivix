Toolbar Tools

The toolbar adapts to the active mode and tool. Some presentation features require Scrivix Unlimited or are included with Scrivix Remote for Education.

IconTool & Description
📸Screenshot

Save a clean image of the canvas or Mac display area to Photos. Scrivix removes its toolbar from the captured image.

👁Show or Hide Ink

Temporarily hide every annotation without deleting anything. Tap again to bring the ink back exactly where it was.

🎨Color Palette

Choose a preset or custom color. Brush and Highlighter each remember their own saved colors. Tap the custom color control for Apple’s color picker, or press and hold it to choose Rainbow or Astro ink.

🖥Overlay & Background Menu

Tap to toggle the current background. Press and hold, then slide or tap to choose white, black, graph paper, lined paper, or a photo. Grid spacing and line width controls appear only when they apply, and your choices are remembered.

〰Brush

Write and draw with pressure-sensitive Apple Pencil ink. Scrivix remembers the Brush size when you switch to another tool and back.

🖍Highlighter

Add translucent emphasis that stays underneath normal ink so writing remains clear. The Highlighter has its own remembered size and color presets.

◇Shape & Line Menu

Press and hold to choose a single-ended arrow, double-ended arrow, straight line, rectangle, or circle. Slide to an option and lift, or tap the option. While drawing a rectangle or circle, use a second finger to lock a perfect square or circle.

💧Fill

Fill Smart Shapes, Shape Tool objects, and many closed hand-drawn shapes. Rainbow produces a smooth diagonal gradient; Astro adds a space-inspired gradient and stars.

✦Laser Pointer

Draw temporary marks that fade away automatically without affecting permanent annotations.

↖Selection

Tap a stroke or drag a selection around several strokes, then move, resize, or delete the selection. Selection is available on the iPad canvas and during Mac-connected annotation sessions.

🧼Object Eraser

Remove an entire stroke or shape by touching the ink. Scrivix uses the object’s actual path so nearby annotations are less likely to be erased accidentally.

🗑Clear

Remove the current page’s annotations while preserving its selected background or overlay. Undo can restore an accidental clear.

🖱Control Mode

Control your Mac from iPad: tap to click, double tap to double-click, press and hold for right-click, drag windows and items, hover Apple Pencil to move the cursor, scroll with two fingers, and pinch or expand to zoom supported apps.

✍Apple Pencil Only

Prevent fingers and your palm from creating ink while keeping supported finger gestures available for navigation and editing.

↩ ↪Undo & Redo

Step backward or forward through drawing, editing, erasing, and clear actions. You can also use the multi-touch shortcuts below.

🎚Tool Size

Choose Small, Medium, or Large and fine-tune the active tool with the slider. Tools that do not use a size control disable these options.

Special Ink & Smart Drawing

FeatureHow It Works
🌈Rainbow Ink

Press and hold the custom color control and choose Rainbow. Colors transition smoothly along your writing and continue from the previous stroke rather than restarting at red every time.

🌌Astro Ink

Choose Astro from the same press-and-hold menu for deep purples and blacks with varied star highlights. Its pattern continues naturally across strokes.

▭Smart Shapes

Draw a rough line, circle, rectangle, triangle, or arrow, then briefly hold Apple Pencil still. Scrivix replaces it with a cleaner shape while preserving the active standard, Rainbow, or Astro style.

⬛Closed Shape Fill

The Fill Tool can color many closed freehand shapes even when you do not convert them into Smart Shapes.

Apple Pencil & Touch Gestures

GestureAction
✌Two-Finger Tap

Undo the last action without reaching for the toolbar.

3Three-Finger Tap

Redo the last undone action.

↕Two-Finger Scroll

Scroll Mac documents, webpages, and presentations. Scrolling remains available while a drawing tool is selected.

↔Two-Finger Page Swipe

In an iPad presentation, swipe horizontally to move between pages. Swipe forward from the final page to add a clean new page.

⌁Pinch & Expand

In Control Mode, pinch to zoom out or expand to zoom in. Scrivix sends the appropriate zoom shortcut to the active Mac app instead of enlarging the video feed.

◌Apple Pencil Hover

Preview toolbar controls and menus before touching the screen. In Control Mode, hover also positions the Mac pointer.

◐Apple Pencil Squeeze

Open the quick color menu with your saved colors near the current Pencil location. The menu moves to remain clear of the toolbar.

↺Apple Pencil Double Tap

Quickly switch between the Brush and Object Eraser.

Pages & Presentations

Build a reusable whiteboard or lesson as a multi-page Scrivix presentation.

FeatureWhat You Can Do
1/4Page Counter

See the current page and total number of pages. Tap the counter to open the Page Organizer.

＋Add & Insert Pages

Add a new page after the current page, including between existing pages. New pages begin with the current background for a natural lesson flow.

▦Page Organizer

Review page thumbnails, jump to a page, duplicate or delete pages, and drag pages into a new order.

▣Independent Page Content

Each page remembers its own ink, background or overlay, and inserted images.

✧New Presentation

Start a blank presentation without leaving the canvas. Scrivix offers to save changes before replacing the current project.

Scalable Images & Layers

FeatureHow It Works
🖼Insert Images

Add one or more images from Photos without replacing the full canvas background.

⤢Move & Resize

Choose Adjust Images, then tap the image you want. Drag directly on that image to move it and use its resize controls with a finger or Apple Pencil. Tap away to clear the selection.

▤Layer Toolbar

Use the thumbnail strip to select and reorder overlapping images. Bring an image forward, send it backward, or drag thumbnails into the exact layer order you need.

✓Done Editing

Tap the clearly marked Done button beside the image control to return to writing and drawing.

Save, Open & Export

FeatureWhat It Does
💾Save Scrivix Presentations

Save editable .scrivix projects to Files, On My iPad, or iCloud Drive. A project keeps its pages, ink, overlays, and images so you can continue later.

📂Open & Continue

Open a saved Scrivix presentation and keep editing, erasing, rearranging, or adding pages.

↧Save & Save As

Update the current project or save a separate copy under a new name. The current file name appears at the top of the canvas.

PDFExport Every Page to PDF

Use the clearly labeled PDF export action from the Page Organizer or save menu to create a shareable PDF containing all presentation pages.

Tip: Save as a Scrivix presentation when you want to edit later. Export as PDF when you want to send, print, or post a finished copy.

Mac-Connected Mode

FeatureHow It Works
🔐Local Pairing

Open the free Scrivix Mac companion, choose the Mac on your iPad, and enter the displayed passcode. Both devices must be on the same local network.

🖥Display Switching

When the Mac has multiple displays, choose the display you want to view and annotate. Scrivix keeps the overlay and video feed aligned with that display.

↖Connected Selection

Use the Selection tool on annotations created during the connected session. Move, resize, or delete strokes and Scrivix updates the corresponding Mac overlay.

✅Mac Permissions

Screen Recording lets Scrivix send the Mac display to iPad. Accessibility lets Control Mode click, scroll, and move the pointer. If a permission appears enabled but does not work, remove Scrivix from that macOS permission list, add it again, and reopen the app.

🔒Private by Design

Screen video, drawing commands, and controls travel over your local network. Scrivix does not send presentation content to a Scrivix cloud account.

AirPlay & External Displays

FeatureWhat Viewers See
▭Clean Canvas Presentation

When you use the iPad canvas with AirPlay or a connected external display, Scrivix presents the writing surface without the editing toolbar so the audience sees only your content.

SPrivate Connection Screen

If the connection screen is open, the external display shows Scrivix branding rather than exposing your list of available Macs or connection details.
